# Stage 2 — Test Checklist

Unity 2022.3.20f1, OpenWorld scene, Play mode:
[ ] 1. PLAY -> Loading -> OpenWorld khulta hai, car road par khari hai, HUD dikhta hai
[ ] 2. ACCEL dabake rakho -> car aage badhti hai, speed badhti hai, gear 1->6 badalta hai, RPM badhta hai
[ ] 3. Accelerator chhodo -> car dheere hoti hai. BRAKE dabao -> rukti hai. Ruki hui car me BRAKE dabake rakho -> reverse chalti hai
[ ] 4. Steering ◀ ▶ dabao -> front wheels mudte hain, car mudti hai. Tez speed par steering halka ho jata hai
[ ] 5. HANDBRAKE dabao -> peeche wale wheels lock, car slide karti hai
[ ] 6. NITRO dabao -> speed zyada badhti hai
[ ] 7. CAM dabao -> 4 cameras badalte hain: Chase, Close Rear, Cockpit, Front
[ ] 8. LIGHT dabao -> headlights on/off
[ ] 9. GET OUT dabao -> banda car se bahar nikalta hai, on-foot controls aate hain, paidal chal sakte ho
[ ] 10. Paidal chal ke car ke paas jao -> ENTER CAR button aata hai, dabao -> dobara driving shuru
[ ] 11. RESPAWN dabao -> car wapas (0,1,0) par aa jati hai
[ ] 12. Garage me ab sirf 5 cars dikhti hain, koi mission / countdown nahi hai
[ ] 13. Console me koi red error nahi hai

Limitations: Traffic abhi nahi hai (Stage 4), full city districts abhi nahi hain (Stage 3), on-foot animation abhi simple hai (walk cycle nahi), car model primitive-based hai.
