# Stage 4 — Test Checklist

Unity 2022.3.20f1, OpenWorld scene, Play mode:
[ ] 1. PLAY -> OpenWorld me traffic cars dikhti hain, roads par chal rahi hain
[ ] 2. 6 types dikhte hain: Sedan, SUV, Van, Sports, Truck, Bus — alag size/colour/speed
[ ] 3. Traffic cars lane me chalti hain, intersection par kabhi mudti hain, kabhi seedha jati hain
[ ] 4. Red light par traffic rukti hai, green par chalti hai — light ki spheres bhi badalti hain
[ ] 5. Aage wali car ke paas jao -> peeche wali slow hoti hai, 6 m par ruk jati hai, takrati nahi
[ ] 6. Player car traffic ke aage ruk jaye to traffic horn bajati hai (3 sec baad)
[ ] 7. Player traffic se takraye to collision hota hai, car rukti / slide hoti hai
[ ] 8. Player door drive karo -> door wali traffic gayab hokar player ke paas dobara spawn hoti hai, hamesha aas paas traffic rehti hai
[ ] 9. Stage 1-3 ka sab kuch abhi bhi kaam karta hai: driving, on-foot, city, minimap, garage 5 cars
[ ] 10. Console me koi red error nahi hai, frame rate stable hai

Limitations: Traffic cars kinematic hain, takkar par woh khud push nahi hoti, sirf player react karta hai. Traffic highway elevated road par nahi chalti, sirf grid roads par. Bus/truck ke 6 wheels visual nahi, 4 hi hain.
